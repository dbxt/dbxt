# David Wayne Baxter (Bax)

Senior Engineering Manager: platform reliability, observability, AI agents. Building [TechDrift](https://techdrift.app/).

## Building

- [TechDrift](https://techdrift.app/) - Tracks what is gaining traction in tech. A topic is promoted only when it holds up across independent sources over time, and every claim links to its evidence.
- [GamingDrift](https://gamingdrift.app/) - The same evidence-first approach, for gaming.
- [SportsDrift](https://sportsdrift.app/) - The same, for sports.

One shared engine runs all three, built and operated solo with AI agents. Stack: Node.js, Postgres, and a self-hosted LLM for bounded advisory tasks. [Case study](https://davidwaynebaxter.com/ai/techdrift/).

## Results

- Replaced the service carrying most of a sportsbook's client data traffic, cutting latency from 30 to 60 seconds to 30 to 60 milliseconds, roughly 1,000 times faster.
- Built 200+ Terraform-generated monitors with automatic P1 paging, so every new endpoint ships with standard coverage.
- Led teams of up to 11 direct reports: six promotions and one voluntary departure over three and a half years.

Skills: platform reliability, observability, Terraform, Datadog, AWS, Java, Kotlin, AI agents.
